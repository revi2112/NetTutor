1.  Introduction

   The protocol moves examples between hosts.

1.1.  Terminology

   An example is a unit of data.

2.  Operation

   Hosts follow these steps:

   1.  Open a connection.

   2.  Send the example.

   3.  Close the connection.

   The steps repeat until done.
